## CHROME — Standard centered header

Cross-brand UI contract (2026-07-21): every nested/pushed navigation screen presents its title in the **standard centered header** (`ScreenHeader` with a `title` + back button, or the equivalent centered header in a shared scaffold), and does **not** repeat that title as a large in-body heading.
